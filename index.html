Welcome to Anup House

A peaceful place to return to after a day in Bodhgaya.

At Anup House, our family has been looking after guests since 2018. We keep things simple: clean, comfortable rooms, a warm welcome, and a helpful local base close to Bodhgaya’s most important places.

Whether you are visiting for pilgrimage, teaching, an event, or quiet time, we are happy to welcome you.

Pilgrimage & teachings

Close to the Bodhi Tree and Mahabodhi Temple.

Bodhgaya is one of the world’s great places of Buddhist pilgrimage. The nearby Mahabodhi Temple Complex, home to the sacred Bodhi Tree, is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Anup House is a practical, family-run base for guests visiting the temple, joining the annual Monlam prayer gathering, or attending teaching and prayer programmes when they take place in Bodhgaya.

For current programme dates and local travel advice, please contact the family before you arrive.
