# Reviews and local listings

Do these after the website is publicly deployed. Keep the same business name, phone number, address and photos everywhere.

## First priority: Google Business Profile

The Google Maps listing for Anup House already exists. Shashi should claim it, complete the owner verification, and add the public website URL once live. Add the current room and exterior photos, phone number, email, and correct opening/arrival information.

## Second priority: Tripadvisor

Create or claim Anup House's accommodation listing through [Tripadvisor Owners](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Owners). Add the public website URL after it is live.

## Optional: Trustpilot

Trustpilot can be useful later, but Tripadvisor and Google reviews will matter more for a guest house. If used, create a real business profile and invite all genuine guests neutrally after their stay.

## Important review rule

Do not post reviews yourself or ask people who have not stayed at Anup House. After a real guest leaves, send a short, neutral message such as:

> Thank you for staying at Anup House. If you have a moment, we would be grateful for an honest review: [review link]

Only add review links to the website once the official profiles are claimed.
